# Restless Markers Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** September 24, 2026

## What Restless Markers does not do

Restless Markers has no user accounts, does not sell personal information, and does not use advertising cookies. It does not send marker searches, selected markers, or precise device location as custom analytics events.

## Aggregate analytics

The public website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate visit and performance statistics. It is configured without analytics cookies or local-storage analytics and without custom events for marker searches, selected markers, or precise location. Cloudflare’s own privacy materials and terms apply to that service.

## Your device and optional features

Layout and last-selected-state preferences stay on your device.

Location, address/city, ZIP-code, and map features operate only when you request them. Your browser may ask for location permission. Restless Markers does not receive or store your precise device location as a custom analytics event.

Some optional features use third-party services, such as map tiles, geocoding/ZIP lookup services, directions links, and linked source pages. When you use one of those features, your browser communicates with that provider under its own privacy practices.

When an HMdb photograph is displayed, the image is requested directly from HMdb.org. HMdb may receive the ordinary information sent by a browser when requesting a web resource, such as your IP address and browser details.

## Feedback

If you email feedback, we receive the information you choose to include in that email. Please do not send sensitive personal information, confidential material, or image files unless asked.
