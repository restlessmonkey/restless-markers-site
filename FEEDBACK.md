# Restless Markers Feedback Guidelines

Feedback helps improve the site. Useful reports include:

- incorrect marker title, text, location, county, or source link;
- an image that does not show the marker or has incorrect credit;
- a missing marker;
- a broken feature or mobile-display issue; or
- an idea that would make the site easier to use.

Email <restlessmonkey.software@gmail.com> with a short description, the state and marker title/number when applicable, and a public source link if available.

Please do not send confidential information, sensitive personal information, copyrighted images, scans, or other files unless requested. You keep ownership of what you send; feedback is handled under the permission described in the [Terms of Use](TERMS-OF-USE.md).

Submitting feedback does not guarantee a response or change, but reports are reviewed before changes are made to published marker data.
