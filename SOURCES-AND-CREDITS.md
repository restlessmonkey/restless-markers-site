# Restless Markers Sources and Credits

Restless Markers is an independent site. Government and third-party sources remain authoritative for their own records and content.

## Marker data

| Area | Primary source role |
|---|---|
| Texas | Texas Historical Commission Historic Sites Atlas |
| North Carolina | North Carolina Department of Natural and Cultural Resources historical-marker GIS/listing |
| California | Historical Marker Database physical-marker inventory; California Office of Historic Preservation enrichment when deterministically matched |
| Michigan | Michigan Department of Natural Resources / Michigan History Center Historical Markers public dataset |
| Virginia | Virginia Department of Historic Resources Highway Markers GIS and official text sources |

## Photographs and linked detail

HMdb.org is an independent historical-marker resource. Where displayed, Texas and Virginia marker photographs are loaded remotely from HMdb.org and retain the displayed photographer credit, HMdb attribution, and source-page link. Restless Markers does not claim ownership of those photographs.

Virginia DHR remains authoritative for Virginia marker identity and inscription text. HMdb photographs are supplemental. The intended future order is a verified marker-specific DHR image first, then HMdb only where no DHR image is available.

## Maps and software

Map display uses OpenStreetMap contributors, Leaflet, and Leaflet.markercluster. Word cloud functionality uses wordcloud2.js. See [Third-Party Notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) for applicable library license notices.
